Dog handlers and private security in the Var

MO'CYNO is based in Toulon. Our dog handlers and security officers guard villas, construction sites, warehouses, ports and events across the Var. We are reachable 24/7 on +33 4 85 29 03 90 or +33 6 66 03 51 16.

Toulon area

Around Toulon, we guard warehouses, business parks and construction sites at night, at weekends and during closures. In the harbour, we work on land only: quays, access to shipyards and guarding of docked vessels from the quay, around the vessel. Our SSIAP fire safety officers work in public-access and high-rise buildings across the area.

Towns: Toulon, La Seyne-sur-Mer, Six-Fours-les-Plages, La Valette-du-Var, La Garde, Ollioules, Le Revest-les-Eaux, Hyères, Bandol.

Gulf of Saint-Tropez

In the Gulf, we guard villas and estates, during the season and over the winter closure, and we secure private receptions and events. On large wooded properties, the dog handler patrols the agreed areas.

Towns: Saint-Tropez, Ramatuelle, Gassin, La Croix-Valmer, Cavalaire-sur-Mer, Grimaud, Port-Grimaud, Sainte-Maxime, Les Issambres, Plan-de-la-Tour.

East Var

In Fréjus and Saint-Raphaël, we guard campsites and holiday residences, shops, construction sites and co-ownership residences, all year round or for the season.

Towns: Fréjus, Saint-Raphaël, Puget-sur-Argens, Roquebrune-sur-Argens, Agay, Les Adrets-de-l'Estérel.

Our services in the Var

K9 security: Dog handler patrols on large grounds and isolated sites.

Guarding and patrols: Static posts, patrols and access control.

Alarm response: Under contract.

Video surveillance: Depending on the site.

SSIAP fire safety: Prevention patrols and security control room.

Event security: Screening and monitoring of restricted areas.

Ports and docked vessels: Quay monitoring and guarding around the vessel, from the quay; no services on board or at sea.

International regattas in Saint-Tropez, 2026: night security for the installations and monitoring of valuable objects on display, with 2 K9 officers.
