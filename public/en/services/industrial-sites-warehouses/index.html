Frequently asked questions

Which sites can be covered?

Industrial sites, warehouses and storage areas in Var, Cannes and the French Riviera, following a review of site needs and instructions.

Can guarding be arranged during closures?

Daytime, night-time, weekend and closure-period guarding can be considered. Coverage hours are agreed for each site.

What does access control cover?

Access for staff, visitors and deliveries, in line with the permissions and instructions defined by the site.

When should dog handlers or SSIAP be considered?

Dog handlers can be considered for large areas and isolated sites. SSIAP fire safety is included where the site requires it.

What information is needed for a quote?

The town, site area, operating hours, access points, stock type and site instructions. Please also specify the closure periods concerned.
